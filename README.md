# GSU Market Hub

## Project Info

**URL**: [GSU Market Hub](https://gsu-market-hub.vercel.app)

## Purpose

GSU Market Hub is a platform designed for students of Gombe State University to facilitate trading and market transactions. It allows users to buy, sell, and exchange items within the campus community, ensuring a safe and efficient trading experience.

## Features

- **Secure Trading**: Verified users and KYC process ensure safe transactions within our community.
- **Student Community**: Connect with fellow GSU students and trade with trust.
- **Market Insights**: Stay updated with trending items and best deals on campus.
- **Direct Messaging**: Chat securely with sellers and buyers within the platform.
- **Smart Search**: Find exactly what you need with our advanced search features.
- **Easy Listings**: List your items for sale in minutes with our streamlined process.
