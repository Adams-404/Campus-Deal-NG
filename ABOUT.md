# About Campus Deal

Campus Deal is a student-first campus platform that combines:
- a trusted **marketplace** for buying and selling,
- **gigs** and opportunity discovery,
- and campus lifestyle modules like events, study resources, and updates.

It is built to make campus life more affordable, connected, and opportunity-driven through verified student participation.

## Quick Links
- Live site: https://campusdeal.ng/
- Main app code: `/src`
- Backend/API and functions: `/api`
- Mobile app (Flutter, WIP): `/mobile`

## Core Product Areas
- Student verification and trust/safety
- Marketplace listings
- Messaging and notifications
- Gigs and campus opportunities
- Settings, help, privacy, and support flows

## Repository Snapshot
- This is a monorepo with web as the current source of truth.
- The Flutter mobile app in `/mobile` mirrors web product flows and is being developed toward standalone extraction.
